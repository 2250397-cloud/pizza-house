About Pizza House

Our Story

Pizza House is a neighborhood pizzeria serving freshly made pizzas. We prepare each order with tasty ingredients and bake it until the crust is golden.

Company Background

  • 2026 - Present: Serving handmade pizzas and family meal bundles in our neighborhood.
  • 2025: Started as a small weekend pop-up focused on fresh dough and local ingredients.
  • Current services: takeaway, delivery coordination, event catering, and custom pizza orders.

Our Mission

To serve delicious food and make every meal a little more enjoyable.

Our Promise

Fresh ingredients, friendly service, and pizza made with care.
